About Our Community

From reactive dogs to confident companions, every member of our community is part of something bigger — a shared journey toward a life they love with their dog.

This is where accountability, adventure, and connection come together. Not just for the dogs. For the people too.

A place where dogs become confident, owners become proud, and training becomes a life you actually want to live.

What We Do

Every meetup is a real-world
training opportunity.

01

Adventure Hikes

Trail environments that challenge dogs and build confidence in natural distractions — wildlife, terrain, off-leash opportunities.

02

Pack Walks

Structured group walks that develop leash skills, dog-to-dog manners, and the ability to move calmly in a pack.

03

Café Socials

The patio experience most owners dream about. Dogs practice calmness and settle skills in busy, real-world social environments.

04

Urban Outings

City streets, markets, and busy public spaces — the environments where real life actually happens.

05

Recall Sessions

Focused off-leash recall training in controlled environments — building the reliability that creates real freedom.

06

Reactivity Sessions

Specialized experiences for dogs working through triggers — structured, safe, and guided by Jenn throughout.
