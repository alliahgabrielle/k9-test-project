01 — Entry Point

Focused
Foundations.

In-person or online

Focused Foundations teaches the communication system that forms the backbone of everything we do at Impackful K9. Owners learn how to create engagement, improve focus, and develop a dog that wants to work with them rather than against them.

Dogs learn how to navigate the world alongside their owners with greater confidence and understanding. When communication improves, doors begin to open. Walks become enjoyable. Patios become possible. Adventures become less stressful.

This is for you if your dog:
  • Pulls on leash
  • Struggles with focus outdoors
  • Becomes easily distracted
  • Is newly adopted with little training
  • Is an adolescent who is a little wild
The Transformation

For many owners, this is the first time they feel truly empowered. Instead of feeling pulled through life by their dog, they begin to feel capable and confident. Focused Foundations provides the roadmap that prepares both dog and owner for everything that comes next.

02 — The Lived Experience

Mindful
Meetups.

Ongoing membership · In-person · Lower Mainland BC

Mindful Meetups is where training becomes a lifestyle. These guided group experiences allow owners and dogs to practice skills in real-world environments while building confidence, community, and lasting relationships.

Most dog training programs end when the lessons are over. Owners are given information and expected to figure out implementation on their own. Mindful Meetups was created to bridge that gap.

Experiences include:
Adventure hikes Pack walks Café socials Urban outings Recall sessions Reactivity sessions Community events
The Transformation

Members aren't simply teaching their dogs to sit or walk politely on leash. They are learning how to enjoy hiking trails together, relax on restaurant patios, walk through busy public spaces, and participate in activities that many dog owners only dream about.

Inside Mindful Meetups

Reactivity Remedy

Specialized sessions for dogs that struggle around triggers — other dogs, people, bicycles, wildlife, or busy environments. Rather than suppressing unwanted behaviours, the goal is to help dogs learn how to process their environment more calmly and successfully.

03 — Intensive

The Mindful
Reset.

Private · Immersive · One dog at a time

Unlike traditional board and train facilities that house dozens of dogs at a time, The Mindful Reset accepts only one client dog at a time. Your dog lives inside my home as a member of the family and receives individualized attention throughout their stay.

The goal is not perfection. The goal is momentum. This program helps establish the habits, communication, and structure that create long-term success.

Program focus:
  • Leash communication
  • Engagement and focus
  • Household routines
  • Public exposure
  • Confidence-building
  • Real-world manners
This is for you if you have:
  • A puppy beginning their training journey
  • An adolescent dog needing structure
  • A dog that pulls or struggles with focus
  • A family wanting to establish strong habits early
Not suitable for:

Severe aggression · Bite histories · Resource guarding · Separation anxiety
