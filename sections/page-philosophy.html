Jenn's Story

I didn't want to create
another dog training business.

I wanted to create a place where people could experience what life feels like when training becomes part of everyday living. Where confidence replaces frustration. Where adventures replace avoidance. Where owners stop feeling embarrassed and start feeling proud.

Most people contact me not really asking for dog training. They're asking if there's hope. Hope that their dog can stop reacting. Hope that walks can become enjoyable again. Hope that life with their dog can become what they imagined it would be.

For more than a decade, I've helped dog owners discover that the answer is yes. Not through shortcuts or quick fixes — but through learning a new set of skills and a different way of looking at the relationship between people and dogs.

What We Believe

Guided by a few
simple beliefs.

These principles shape every program, every meetup, and every experience we create. They're not rules — they're the foundation of how we work.

01

Calmness is created through clarity.

Clear communication, clear expectations. When both the person and the dog understand what's being asked, calmness follows naturally.

02

Confidence is built through repetition.

There are no shortcuts. Lasting change comes from consistent practice in the real environments where life actually happens.

03

Community accelerates growth.

When you're surrounded by people who understand your journey, progress accelerates. Connection between owners is as important as the training itself.

04

Nature is an ideal classroom.

Trails, parks, beaches, and cafés aren't distractions from training — they're the point. Real environments produce real results.

05

Both dogs and humans thrive together.

When we support both the person and the dog together, the relationship changes. And when the relationship changes, an entirely new life together becomes possible.

What Makes This Different

Most trainers focus
on training dogs.

I focus on developing dogs and people together. Because when both are supported, the relationship changes.

That's why you'll often find me teaching on trails, in parks, cafés, and public spaces throughout Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Because life doesn't happen in a training facility. It happens in the real world.

Not perfect obedience. Not flawless behaviour. A life well lived together — for both you and your dog.
