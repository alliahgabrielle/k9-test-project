# Coastal Cathedral — Combined Direction Brief

## Concept
Where the seawall meets the old-growth grove. The hybrid direction blends **Coastal Fog**'s airy, misty palette with **Mossy Cathedral**'s grounded forest depth — capturing the Pacific Northwest's defining edge, where cedars descend to the tideline. Cinematic, atmospheric, deeply West Coast.

## Tagline / Positioning
*"Grounded in the grove. Open to the shore."*

## Combined Color Palette
| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Cedar | #2D3A2E | Primary dark — forest foundation |
| Moss | #5B6B4A | Secondary — earth, living |
| Cedar Bark | #6B4A3A | Warm anchor — rooted |
| Sea Glass | #B8D4CE | Accent — tide, spaciousness |
| Mist White | #F0EDE8 | Background — breath |
| Driftwood | #C4B5A2 | Neutral bridge — tactile |

Dark forest tones ground the system; coastal mist and sea-glass lift it. The bridge color (Driftwood) belongs to both worlds.

## Typography (locked)
- Headers: THE SEASONS BOLD, ALL CAPS, 80pt
- Subheaders: Montserrat Medium, 40pt
- Body: Montserrat Regular, 25pt

## Cinematic Visual Direction
- Forest and shore in dialogue — every image carries both worlds or shows the moment of transition
- Atmospheric, painterly, layered depth
- Overcast natural light, hazy compression
- Real moments at the edge between environments
- Negative space honored
- No bright color, no staging, no obedience-poster energy

## Three Brand Guides to Deliver (4 sheets each, 12 total, 16:9)
For each direction:
1. **Cover & Logo System** — title sheet, hero photograph, 3 logo lockup variants
2. **Color & Typography** — extended palette with HEX + usage, three-tier type hierarchy
3. **Visual Language** — 6-image photography direction grid with captions
4. **Applications** — 4–6 photorealistic mockups (business card, tote, bandana, signage, social tile, mug)

**Guides:**
- A. COASTAL FOG (standalone) — light/airy palette only
- B. MOSSY CATHEDRAL (standalone) — deep forest palette only
- C. COASTAL CATHEDRAL (combined) — blended palette, the hybrid system
